About Me

An engineer who enjoys bringing people and plans together.

I've spent my career on the projects that shape cities: rail lines, utility networks, towers and mixed-use communities.

I started as a structural engineer in Cairo and Doha, then moved up to leading full delivery teams with ECG, Wood, WSP and The Muzzo Group. Today I lead design management on Diriyah Gate II in Riyadh, coordinating more than 185 engineers across five countries.

My job is to keep everyone aligned. I clarify what the client needs, untangle the interfaces between disciplines, and keep cost, schedule and change control honest. I've worked on both the consultant side and the developer side, and that helps me see problems early.
