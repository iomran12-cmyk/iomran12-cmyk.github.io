About me

I started with concrete and calculations. Now I run the whole show.

I've spent my career on the projects that shape cities: rail lines, utility networks, towers and mixed-use communities.

I began as a structural engineer in Cairo and Doha, then went on to lead full delivery teams with ECG, Wood, WSP and The Muzzo Group. Today I lead design management on Diriyah Gate II in Riyadh, with more than 185 engineers across five countries.

The job, as I see it, is to keep everyone aligned. I clarify what the client actually needs, untangle the interfaces between disciplines, and keep cost, schedule and change control honest. I've sat on both sides of the table, consultant and developer, so I tend to spot problems early.
